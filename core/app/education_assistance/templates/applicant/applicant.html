APPLICATION FOR

EDUCATIONAL

ASSISTANCE

MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 677-2025

Qualification/s

  • The Applicant or his/her parents/guardian must be a resident and/or registered voter of the municipality of Labo Camarines Norte.
  • The Applicant (Junior and Senior High School) is enrolled in any educational institution within the province while the College student Applicant is enrolled in any state colleges or universities within the province of Camarines Norte.
  • With at least 80 percent weighted average.
  • The Family is not a beneficiary of 4P's Program.
  • For Those passer of Alternative Learning System (ALS) program, item No. 3 hereof is waived.

Requirement/s

  • Certificates of Grades reflecting the general weighted average or photocopy of the Report Card except for ALS passer-applicants.
  • ALS passer applicants shall present the A & E results.
  • Barangay Residency stating that the parents/guardians of the grantee are residents of the barangay and stating further that they belong to indigent families.
  • Self declaration of parent/guardian that their family is not a recipient of 4Ps program subscribe before the Municipal Mayor.
  • Certificate of Enrollment or Matriculation Form, whatever is applicable.

Data Privacy Notice & Terms and Conditions

Data Privacy Notice

The Municipality of Labo, Camarines Norte, through its Education Assistance Program, collects personal information solely for the purpose of processing your scholarship application, verification, and disbursement of financial assistance.

Information Collected: Full name, contact details, educational information, guardian details, address, and 4Ps beneficiary status.

Purpose:

  • To evaluate eligibility for educational assistance
  • To communicate program updates and requirements
  • To maintain records for monitoring and reporting
  • To comply with government auditing and legal requirements

Data Protection: Your personal information is stored securely and access is limited to authorized personnel only. We do not share your data with third parties except when required by law.

Your Rights: You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012).

Terms and Conditions

  • All information provided must be true and accurate. False declaration may result in disqualification or legal action.
  • Recipients must maintain good academic standing and comply with program guidelines.
  • The Municipality reserves the right to verify submitted information.
  • Assistance may be discontinued for non-compliance with requirements.
  • By submitting this application, you consent to the processing of your personal data as described above.

For inquiries, contact the Education Assistance Program Coordinator or the System Administrator.
